Data: MIMIC-IV (Medical Information Mart for Intensive Care IV)

MIMIC-IV is a de-identified database of patients admitted to the critical care units of
Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center. It is hosted on PhysioNet:
https://physionet.org/content/mimiciv/

Access is restricted. To use it you must:
  1. create a PhysioNet account and complete credentialing,
  2. complete the required human-subjects research training, and
  3. sign the data use agreement for the dataset.

The data use agreement does not allow sharing the data, so no MIMIC-IV records are
included in this repository. After you have access, build merged_data.csv (and
merged_data_raceincluded.csv for the demographic split) using the preprocessing code in scripts/,
then update DEFAULT_PATH at the top of each notebook.
